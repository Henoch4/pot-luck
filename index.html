How a circle turns

1

Create a circle

Pick a contribution amount, the number of rounds and the member list.

2

Contribute each round

Every member pays the fixed amount into the contract, once per round.

3

Collect the pot

When all have paid, that round's member receives the pot. Order is fixed at creation.

Why Potluck

Contract custody

Contributions sit in the contract between rounds, so nobody can run with the pot.

Deterministic payouts

When the last member of a round pays, the contract sends the pot in the same block.

Fully verifiable

Every contribution and payout is an explorer transaction, public and auditable.
